## 1. Physical Chemistry Core


![Chemistry Physical Kinetics And Electrochemistry](/media/chemistry_physical_kinetics_and_electrochemistry.webp)
*Description: Two-panel physical chemistry reference: (Panel A) Integrated rate laws of zero and first order kinetics with half-life trajectories; (Panel B) Electrochemistry, Nernst equation, Kohlrausch's law, and colligative property formulations.*


### 1.1 Atomic Structure & Quantum Mechanics
* **Bohr's Orbit Quantization:** $mvr = \frac{nh}{2\pi}$. Radius: $r_n = 0.529 \frac{n^2}{Z}\text{ \AA}$.
* Velocity: $v_n = 2.18 \times 10^6 \frac{Z}{n}\text{ m/s}$. Energy: $E_n = -13.6 \frac{Z^2}{n^2}\text{ eV/atom}$.
* **de Broglie Wavelength:** $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv} = \frac{h}{\sqrt{2mE_k}} = \frac{12.27}{\sqrt{V}}\text{ \AA}$ (for electrons).
* **Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle:** $\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \ge \frac{h}{4\pi}$.


### 1.2 Stoichiometry & Equivalent Concept
* Mole Map: $\text{Moles} = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Molar Mass}} = \frac{\text{Volume at STP (L)}}{22.4} = \frac{N}{N_A}$.
* Equivalent Weight: $E = \frac{M}{n\text{-factor}}$.
  * Acids: $n = \text{Basicity}$ (number of replaceable $H^+$).
  * Bases: $n = \text{Acidity}$ (number of replaceable $OH^-$).
  * Redox agents: $n = \text{Total change in oxidation state per mole of reactant}$.
* Normality: $N = \text{Molarity} \times n\text{-factor}$. Equivalents $= N \times V\text{(L)}$.


### 1.3 Thermodynamics & Thermochemistry
* First Law: $\Delta U = q + w$ ($w = -P_{\text{ext}}\Delta V$).
* Enthalpy: $H = U + PV \implies \Delta H = \Delta U + \Delta n_g RT$.
* Second Law & Entropy: $\Delta S = \frac{q_{\text{rev}}}{T}$. Gibbs Free Energy: $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$.
  * Spontaneity: $\Delta G < 0$ at constant $T, P$.
  * Equilibrium: $\Delta G^\circ = -2.303 RT \log_{10} K_{\text{eq}}$.


### 1.4 Chemical & Ionic Equilibrium
* $K_p = K_c (RT)^{\Delta n_g}$. Reaction quotient $Q < K \implies$ forward shift.
* pH of Weak Acid: $\text{pH} = \frac{1}{2}[\text{p}K_a - \log C]$ ($\alpha = \sqrt{K_a/C}$).
* **Buffer Solutions (Henderson-Hasselbalch):**
  $$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log\left(\frac{[\text{Conjugate Base}]}{[\text{Acid}]}\right)$$
* Hydrolysis of Salts:
  * Weak Acid + Strong Base: $\text{pH} = 7 + \frac{1}{2}\text{p}K_a + \frac{1}{2}\log C$.
  * Weak Base + Strong Acid: $\text{pH} = 7 - \frac{1}{2}\text{p}K_b - \frac{1}{2}\log C$.
  * Weak Acid + Weak Base: $\text{pH} = 7 + \frac{1}{2}\text{p}K_a - \frac{1}{2}\text{p}K_b$.


### 1.5 Electrochemistry & Kinetics
* Nernst Equation: $E_{\text{cell}} = E_{\text{cell}}^\circ - \frac{0.0591}{n}\log_{10} Q$.
* Kohlrausch's Law: $\Lambda_m^\circ = \nu_+ \lambda_+^\circ + \nu_- \lambda_-^\circ$.
* Chemical Kinetics Integrated Laws:
  * Zero Order: $[A] = [A]_0 - kt, \quad t_{1/2} = \frac{[A]_0}{2k}$.
  * First Order: $\ln[A] = \ln[A]_0 - kt \implies t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k}$.
  * Arrhenius Equation: $k = A e^{-E_a/RT} \implies \log_{10}\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right) = \frac{E_a}{2.303 R}\left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right)$.

![Chemistry Inorganic Bonding And Coordination Polyhedra](/media/chemistry_inorganic_bonding_and_coordination_polyhedra.webp)

### 2.1 Chemical Bonding & Periodicity
* **Periodic Trends Inversions:**
  * Ionization Enthalpy: $\text{Be} > \text{B}$ ($2s^2 > 2p^1$), $\text{N} > \text{O}$ ($2p^3 > 2p^4$).
  * Electron Gain Enthalpy: $\text{Cl} > \text{F}$ (-349 vs. -328 kJ/mol due to small 2p size of F).
* **VSEPR Steric Number & Shapes:**
  * S.N. 2: Linear ($sp$). S.N. 3: Trigonal planar ($sp^2$).
  * S.N. 4: Tetrahedral ($sp^3$) or Square planar ($dsp^2$).
  * S.N. 5: Trigonal bipyramidal ($sp^3d$). S.N. 6: Octahedral ($sp^3d^2$).
* **Fajan's Rules for Covalency:** Small cation, large anion, high ionic charge, pseudo-inert gas core ($18e^-$) favors polarization and covalent character.


### 2.2 Coordination Chemistry & Metallurgy
* Werner's Theory: Primary valency (ionizable, oxidation state); Secondary valency (non-ionizable, coordination number).
* **Crystal Field Splitting:**
  * Octahedral ($\Delta_o$): $t_{2g}$ orbitals ($-0.4\Delta_o$) and $e_g$ orbitals ($+0.6\Delta_o$).
  * Tetrahedral ($\Delta_t$): $\Delta_t = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_o$.
* **Spin-Only Magnetic Moment:** $\mu = \sqrt{n(n + 2)}\text{ BM}$ ($n = \text{unpaired electrons}$).
* **Ellingham Diagram:** Thermal reduction feasibility ($\Delta G^\circ < 0$). Lower metal reduces the oxide of an upper metal.


### 2.3 Qualitative Analysis Summary
* Cation Groups:
  * Group I: $\text{Pb}^{2+}, \text{Ag}^+, \text{Hg}_2^{2+}$ (Ppt as Chlorides with dil. $\text{HCl}$).
  * Group II: $\text{Cu}^{2+}, \text{Bi}^{3+}, \text{Cd}^{2+}, \text{As}^{3+}$ (Ppt as Sulphides in acidic $\text{H}_2\text{S}$).
  * Group III: $\text{Fe}^{3+}, \text{Al}^{3+}, \text{Cr}^{3+}$ (Ppt as Hydroxides with $\text{NH}_4\text{OH} + \text{NH}_4\text{Cl}$).
  * Group IV: $\text{Zn}^{2+}, \text{Mn}^{2+}, \text{Ni}^{2+}, \text{Co}^{2+}$ (Ppt as Sulphides in alkaline $\text{H}_2\text{S}$).
  * Group V: $\text{Ba}^{2+}, \text{Sr}^{2+}, \text{Ca}^{2+}$ (Ppt as Carbonates with $(\text{NH}_4)_2\text{CO}_3$).

### 3.1 Electronic Effects & Intermediates (GOC)
* **Inductive Effect:**
  $$-I: -\text{NO}_2 > -\text{CN} > -\text{COOH} > -\text{F} > -\text{Cl} > -\text{Br} > -\text{I} > -\text{OR} > -\text{OH} > -\text{Ph}$$
  $$+I: -\text{O}^- > -\text{COO}^- > -\text{C}(\text{CH}_3)_3 > -\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)_2 > -\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_3 > -\text{CH}_3 > -\text{T} > -\text{D} > -\text{H}$$
* **Aromaticity Criteria (Hückel's Rule):** Cyclic, planar, completely conjugated ring with $(4n + 2)\pi$ electrons ($2, 6, 10, 14\dots$). Antiaromatic: $4n\pi$ electrons (planar, conjugated, highly unstable).
* **Intermediate Stability:**
  * Carbocation: $\text{Tropylium} > \text{CPM} > 3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ > \text{Methyl}$.
  * Carbanion: $\text{Cyclopentadienyl}^- > \text{Benzyl}^- > \text{Allyl}^- > \text{CH}_3^- > 1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ$.
  * Haloform: $\text{CCl}_3^- > \text{CF}_3^-$ ($2p-3d$ backbonding into chlorine).


### 3.2 Key Reaction Mechanisms & Conversions
* **$S_N1$ vs. $S_N2$:**
  * $S_N1$: Two-step via carbocation; racemization; tertiary substrates; polar protic solvents.
  * $S_N2$: Concerted single-step; 100% Walden inversion; methyl & primary substrates; polar aprotic solvents.
* **Named Carbonyl Transformations:**
  * **Aldol Condensation:** Carbonyl with $\alpha\text{-H} + \text{dil. NaOH} \to \beta\text{-hydroxy carbonyl} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \alpha,\beta\text{-unsaturated carbonyl}$.
  * **Cannizzaro Reaction:** Carbonyl lacking $\alpha\text{-H} + \text{conc. NaOH} \to \text{Alcohol} + \text{Carboxylate salt}$ (Disproportionation).
  * **Grignard Reagent ($\text{RMgX}$):** With $\text{HCHO} \to 1^\circ\text{ alcohol}$; With $\text{R}'\text{CHO} \to 2^\circ\text{ alcohol}$; With $\text{R}'\text{COR}'' \to 3^\circ\text{ alcohol}$.
  * **Reimer-Tiemann Reaction:** $\text{Phenol} + \text{CHCl}_3 + \text{NaOH} \to \text{Salicylaldehyde}$ (Dichlorocarbene $:\text{CCl}_2$ intermediate).
