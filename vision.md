# Project Nomad - Website Vision

## Core Purpose
This website is designed to be the ultimate, comprehensive guide for JEE Main preparation. It will explain all core concepts, decode JEE Main Previous Year Question (PYQ) patterns, provide relevant JEE questions, and serve all the critical information a student needs for any particular topic, chapter, or subject.

## Design Philosophy & Pedagogy
**Laser-Focused Learning:**
The core philosophy of the website is to eliminate distractions and cognitive overload by focusing on *one thing at a time*.

For every single learning node, the structure must strictly follow:
1. **One Topic:** A highly specific, granular concept.
2. **One Explanation:** A clear, concise, and rigorous theoretical breakdown.
3. **One Diagram:** A visual representation (graph, geometry, or schematic) to anchor the concept visually.
4. **One Animation/Interactive Element:** A dynamic visual to properly explain the underlying mechanics or changes over time.

## Execution Strategy
- **Granularity:** Chapters will be broken down into their smallest atomic topics.
- **Pattern Recognition:** PYQs will not just be listed, but their *patterns* will be analyzed and explained alongside the theory.
- **Visual-First:** Text will be supported by precise visuals, relying on the existing media vault and future animations to ensure deep understanding.

## Navigation & User Experience
- **Omnibar Search (Ctrl+K):** The primary mode of navigation will be a powerful, Obsidian-style search menu accessible via `Ctrl+K`. Users can instantly search for any specific concept, chapter, or keyword.
- **Concept Deep-Dive:** Selecting a concept from the search will immediately open its dedicated page, bypassing unnecessary hierarchical menus.

## Content Format & Structure
- **Micro-Explanations:** Explanations must be ultra-concise. Avoid large, intimidating paragraphs. Use short, punchy sentences that get straight to the point.
- **Immediate Application:** Every concept explanation must be immediately followed by its direct application in actual Previous Year Questions (PYQs), showing exactly *where* and *how* the concept is tested in the exam.
